release: v0.1.24 - dedicated Home Assistant sub-tab with HA setup help

Added:
- Exports & integrations now has a third sub-tab, "Home Assistant",
  hosting the MQTT block previously grouped with Prometheus and
  InfluxDB under Metrics. The new tab also includes a collapsible
  in-app walkthrough explaining the HA-side configuration:
  install Mosquitto broker add-on, create an MQTT user, add the
  MQTT integration in HA, point this form at the broker, and the
  GPU sensors auto-appear via HA discovery. EN + FR translations.
