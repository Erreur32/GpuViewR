release: v0.1.27 - active indicator dot on Exports sub-tabs

Added:
- Green status dot rendered on each Exports sub-tab label when
  the relevant exporter(s) are active: Notification (webhook
  enabled), Home Assistant (MQTT enabled AND broker connected
  via info.mqtt.connected), Metrics (at least one of Prometheus
  / InfluxDB enabled). The dot has a tooltip ("Active" / "Active
  and connected") and disappears otherwise. EN + FR translations.
