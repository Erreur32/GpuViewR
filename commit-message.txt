release: v0.9.9 skip short-lived pids in the fdinfo scan

- Fixed: short-lived host pids each cost one AppArmor ptrace denial
  before landing in the denied cache (~20/min on a busy host). The
  fdinfo scan now only tries pids alive for at least 10 s.
- Fixed: the fdinfo warning and compose comments blamed other UIDs.
  AppArmor docker-default denies unconfined (host) peers only, other
  containers stay readable with CAP_SYS_PTRACE, which must stay.
