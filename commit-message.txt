release: v0.8.10 dashboard N/A states

Fixes a real UX inconsistency raised by user on .210 (AMD card
with no fan exposed): the "Live" dot in the green corner of each
GaugeCard kept blinking at 1 Hz on the Fan card even though the
displayed value was "N/A". The dot's intent is "this metric is
live"; in practice it was flashing on every WS sample arrival
regardless of whether the metric field was null.

What changed
- src/components/dashboard/GaugeCard.tsx: new optional prop
  `available?: boolean` (default true). When false:
  * the live-tick `useEffect` early-returns (no flash)
  * the resolved colorVar short-circuits to var(--gv-text-dim)
    so the arc fill, central value, AND the dot all go gray
  * the dot opacity drops to 0.3 and the tooltip becomes "N/A on
    this host" instead of "Live"
- src/components/dashboard/Dashboard.tsx: every GaugeCard call
  site (util / mem / fan / temp / power) now passes
  `available={active.X != null}` for its metric. As a side bonus,
  temperature and power got the same `?? 0` + displayValue='N/A'
  treatment as the others, which prevents a TypeError on hosts
  where those fields can be null (Windows PDH, some embedded
  iGPUs). Memory's available also gates on `memory_total` being
  set, since the % needs both.
- src/components/fleet/HostCard.tsx: drop the relative-time
  "il y a Xs" label from the status pill on /fleet cards. The
  cards now read just `● En ligne`. Achieved by passing
  `lastSeenEpoch={null}` to StatusPill (HostsSettingsTab was
  already doing this). User feedback: the seconds counter was
  visual noise on a dense grid where dot color and sparkline
  freshness already cover the staleness signal.

User-visible effect
- AMD passive iGPU (no fan): the Fan card now shows "N/A" in gray
  and its dot stays still. Previously the dot blinked green at
  1 Hz, suggesting a measurement that didn't exist.
- Windows PDH host (no temp/power): same treatment on those two
  cards. They were also crashing rendering before this fix on
  null values; now they degrade cleanly.
