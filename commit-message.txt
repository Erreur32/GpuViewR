release: v0.9.8 stop fdinfo audit storm on AMD agents

- Fixed: the DRM fdinfo scan retried ptrace-gated
  /proc/<pid>/fdinfo reads for every other-UID pid on each tick.
  AppArmor docker-default denies them even with CAP_SYS_PTRACE,
  one kernel audit record each, overflowing the audit backlog.
  Denied pids are now tried once and skipped until they exit,
  with a single startup warning.
