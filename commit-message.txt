release: v0.1.34 - mockGpu uses crypto.randomInt instead of Math.random

Changed:
- mockGpu.ts replaces all four Math.random() calls (sample
  jitter, fake CPU%/GPU%, process memory weights) with a small
  rand01() helper backed by node:crypto.randomInt. Resolves
  SonarCloud's typescript:S2245 PRNG hotspot without scattering
  NOSONAR comments. Behaviour is identical; mock collector still
  only runs under MOCK_GPU=1.
