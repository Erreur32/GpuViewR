release: v0.1.22 - rolling chart window for every range

Fixed:
- Chart's left edge now slides forward for every range, not just
  "live". Previously 5m/15m/1h/6h/24h/3d kept the fetch's initial
  left boundary fixed and just appended new live data, so the
  visible window grew beyond the selected period (e.g. "5m" opened
  at 14:00 was showing 13:55-14:10 by 14:10). A client-side
  rangeToSeconds() mirrors the server's parseRange and the cutoff
  is applied uniformly across all ranges.
