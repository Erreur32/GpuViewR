release: v0.8.17 stop FleetChart tripling /gpu/history requests on poll

The v0.8.16 periodic-poll fix re-fetched each host/GPU's history once
per metric (utilization, temperature, power) even though the endpoint
already returns all three in one response. Repeating that 3x waste
every 15-120s indefinitely produced request/DB bursts that could delay
other server work (including WS heartbeats), making hosts intermittently
flicker to "lagging" on the Fleet page. FleetChart now fetches each
host/GPU's history once per poll and derives all three metrics from
that single response.
