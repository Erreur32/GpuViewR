release: v0.8.23 fix the "lagging" flicker (browser/hub clock skew)

- Root cause of the random "en retard" pill flicker on Settings ->
  Hosts and the fleet views, chased since v0.8.16: the online/lagging
  computation compared hub-stamped last_seen and agent-stamped sample
  timestamps against the browser's own Date.now(). A workstation ~24 s
  ahead of its hub sat right on the 25 s threshold and flipped at
  random on every re-render (one per incoming sample, from any host),
  which is also why one enrolled agent looked fine while two flickered.
- Live freshness is now the browser-clock time the WS frame was
  received (gpuStore.receivedAtByHost), not the agent's timestamp, so
  it is immune to clock skew by construction.
- GET /api/hosts returns the hub's `now`; the UI measures the clock
  offset and shifts every hub-stamped last_seen into the browser clock
  before comparing. Also fixes the "il y a 25s" label that was wrong by
  the same amount.
- Settings -> Hosts shows a warning banner when browser and hub clocks
  disagree by more than 5 s, so the drift gets fixed at the source
  (NTP) instead of being silently masked.
- The temporary [lag-flicker] console diagnostic from v0.8.19 is
  removed.
